## Conventions

- **JPEG** for photographs, **PNG** for anything with text or diagrams.
- Long edge ≤ 2000 px. These are documentation images; a 12 MP phone photo makes the repo
  large for no benefit.
- Annotate the ones that teach something (pin locations, wiring) rather than relying on the
  caption.
- Keep total image weight under a few MB.
